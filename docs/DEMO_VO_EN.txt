TabWrit gives merchants and AI agents a capped, expiring, revocable spending Tab. A prompt injection hits the contract, not your wallet.

The owner funds a vault once. Every owner action is signed in their own wallet. The server never holds the owner key.

For each merchant or agent, the owner opens a Tab: a per-charge cap, a period budget, a lifetime cap, an expiry, and a receiver policy. The spender gets a Function-Call key scoped to charge. It cannot withdraw, pause, revoke, or change the rule.

The customer does not sign again. The merchant's scoped key signs a NEP-366 delegate off-chain, and the relayer pays the gas. The merchant's NEAR balance stays unchanged. The contract still enforces the caps and consumes the nonce.

These are not simulated results. Twenty-three adversarial checks are real testnet transactions. The final vault has zero access keys. There is no deployer backdoor.
